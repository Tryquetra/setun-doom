Boolean 3-Cube Q3 (Left Stage)

Each corner is a 3-bit hardware word, and every cube edge connects codes that differ by strictly 1 bit. The golden ring loops across 6 corners (001-011-010-110-111-101), placing zero (000) as a central hub and locking 100 as an explicit fault sentinel.

Zero-Sum Hex Plane (Right Stage)

All 7 valid geometric states satisfy q + r + s = 0. A 60-degree turn swaps a +1 and a 0 axis (two half-swings, zero full-swings). Encoding those 3 trits individually needs 6 or 9 wires; packing the constrained invariant into HexGray3 uses 3 wires (87.5% state efficiency).

Scope & Honest Frontier

Ideal 0.25x vs Binary 1.00x per wire
